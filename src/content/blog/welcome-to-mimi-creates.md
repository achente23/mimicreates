Hi, I'm Mimi — welcome to my little corner of the internet! 🐥

Mimi Creates started the way most good things do: with a doodle. I've always
loved drawing cute animals, and when I saw how much joy a simple printed
matching game brought to the little ones around me, something clicked. What if
learning materials could feel *handmade*? Soft colors, friendly faces, and
activities that a parent and a toddler can enjoy together at the kitchen
table — no screens required.

## What I make

Everything in the shop is designed by hand, then turned into a printable PDF
you can download instantly:

- **Busy books** — cut-and-play quiet books that keep small hands busy while
  they learn shapes, colors, and first words.
- **Worksheets** — tracing, counting, and early writing practice that feels
  like play, not homework.
- **Coloring pages** — thick, toddler-friendly outlines starring the cutest
  animal friends (including a certain duckling in a bonnet 🦆).
- **Flashcards** — first words and alphabet cards in a soft pastel palette.

## Why printables?

I love that a printable is *yours forever*. Print it once for a rainy
afternoon, print it again for a road trip, laminate your favorites and use
them for years. It's affordable, instant, and there's no shipping — wherever
you are in the world, Mimi Creates can be on your table in five minutes.

## What's next

This blog is where I'll share new releases, free activity ideas, printing
tips, and little peeks behind the scenes of running a tiny art shop. If
there's something you'd love to see — a theme, an animal, a specific
activity — [send me a custom art request](/custom-art/). I read every single
one.

Thank you for being here at the very beginning. Let's learn, play, and create
together! 💛
