You bought a printable busy book — yay! 🎉 Here's exactly how to turn that
PDF into a sturdy, toddler-proof activity book. It takes about 30 minutes,
and you don't need any special equipment (though a laminator helps).

## What you'll need

- Your busy book PDF (downloaded from Etsy after purchase)
- A color printer (or a print shop — see the tip below)
- Cardstock or regular paper
- Scissors, a laminator *or* self-sealing laminating pouches
- Velcro dots (the small transparent ones work best)
- A binder ring or a small folder

## Step 1 — Print

Print the pages at **100% scale** ("Actual size" in your print dialog) on
A4 or US Letter. Cardstock (160–200 gsm) makes pages noticeably sturdier;
regular paper is fine if you're laminating.

> **No color printer at home?** Most print shops will print a PDF for cents
> per page — and the quality is often better than a home inkjet.

## Step 2 — Laminate

Laminate both the pages and the cut-out pieces *before* cutting the pieces
out. Cutting after laminating seals the edges and keeps everything
splash-proof (very important around toddlers and juice cups).

## Step 3 — Cut

Cut out the activity pieces. You don't need to be precise — a rounded,
imperfect edge is actually safer for small hands. If your little one is old
enough, cutting the simple shapes together is an activity in itself.

## Step 4 — Velcro

Stick the **hook** side of the velcro dots on the pages and the **loop**
(soft) side on the pieces. Keeping this consistent means every piece works on
every page — future you will be grateful.

## Step 5 — Bind

Punch a hole in the corner and use a binder ring, or slide the pages into a
slim folder. Ring-bound books let you swap pages in and out, so you can
rotate activities and keep the book feeling new.
